Apple distinguishes readable off-device retention from handling needed to service a request; linked identifiers require separate consideration. [Apple disclosure guidance](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/app-privacy-details/).

Google requires ephemeral off-device processing in form responses and describes an exception for content unreadable outside the sender/recipient because of end-to-end encryption. Neither exception covers independently readable routing/account metadata. [Google Data safety guidance](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10787469).

September 27 owner decision: all officially eligible territories, with no total SMS spending ceiling. Public-mode abuse controls retain only HMAC digests of number indexes, signed device keys and trusted source networks; counters expire within 24 hours and are pruned on requests and a one-minute timer. This mode is implemented separately from live tester admission and is not yet activated. Include its actual deployed metadata processing in final disclosures.
